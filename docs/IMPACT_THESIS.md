# Impact Thesis

CareOS should return time to care without making clinical information less trustworthy.

A real-world win requires both:

- less clinician friction reconstructing, checking and coordinating patient context;
- no material degradation in errors, pending-item detection, provenance, source verification, privacy or clinician authority.

The product must make uncertainty and failure visible. Commercial success cannot override the safety-gated Time Returned to Care objective.
