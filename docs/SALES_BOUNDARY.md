# Sales Truth Boundary

Sales material may say:

- CareOS has source-linked clinical truth/provenance architecture and synthetic engineering evidence;
- it can be evaluated in bounded workflow proof sprints and governed pilots;
- the first workflow target is clinician review/documentation preparation;
- production claims require external clinical, privacy, security, integration and governance evidence.

Sales material may not say:

- CareOS is clinically validated or production-ready today;
- the current model can safely make patient-care decisions;
- synthetic timing/accuracy results are measured hospital outcomes;
- a hospital buying a pilot has approved production PHI use or write-back;
- faster workflow alone is success if verification/safety worsens.
