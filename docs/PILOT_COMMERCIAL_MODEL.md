# CareOS — Ethical Pilot Commercial Model

Status: **commercial hypothesis for pre-production evidence work**. This is not a claim of clinical validation, certification or production readiness.

CareOS should earn revenue from hospitals for reducing integration/evaluation friction and building trustworthy evidence. It should not earn revenue from patient data, clinical uncertainty or unsafe automation.

## Current truth boundary

CareOS is still pre-hospital research with synthetic/deidentified evaluation. The current production G1 remains blocked; paid work must therefore be sold as **discovery, evaluation, integration feasibility, assurance and governed pilot work**, not as a production clinical decision system.

## Who pays

- hospitals and health systems;
- hospital groups/shared IT organisations;
- research/innovation programmes sponsoring governed evaluation;
- interoperability/integration partners where CareOS reduces repeated integration work.

Patients and individual clinicians should not be charged to access their clinical information or participate in normal care.

## What a hospital can legitimately buy now

### 1. CareOS Workflow Proof Sprint

Purpose: determine whether one bounded workflow is worth a governed pilot.

Deliverables:

- workflow map and baseline measurement plan;
- non-secret hospital capability manifest;
- data/source inventory and connector feasibility;
- safety/authority boundary review;
- synthetic/deidentified paired clinician sessions where permitted;
- documented blockers and evidence ledger;
- go / no-go / evidence-needed recommendation.

**Initial price hypothesis:** fixed-fee **€4,900–€9,900** depending on workflow and access. Validate with real buyers before treating this as market pricing.

### 2. Governed Shadow Pilot

Purpose: test a bounded clinician workflow without production write-back or autonomous clinical decisions.

Deliverables may include:

- approved deidentified/vendor sandbox integration;
- role/access and audit configuration;
- source-linked context flow;
- clinician study execution;
- safety-stop and provenance analysis;
- before/after workflow evidence;
- independent reviewer pack;
- pilot closeout report.

**Initial price hypothesis:** **€15k–€35k** fixed pilot scope, plus explicitly agreed third-party/integration costs.

### 3. Read-only Hospital Pilot

Only after governance/technical gates are met.

Commercial unit should be a **bounded site/workflow deployment**, not patient count or clinical decision volume.

Potential pricing after proof:

`implementation/integration + annual site/workflow support licence + assurance/support`

No production price should be finalised before the first real governed pilot shows actual integration burden and measurable value.

## What is never the product

- sale of PHI or patient-derived profiles;
- advertising based on diagnoses, medications or care needs;
- model training on identifiable clinical data without explicit governed legal basis and approval;
- payment for recommending more tests, drugs or procedures;
- pricing tied to denying care or suppressing clinician review;
- charging per AI suggestion in a way that rewards unnecessary generation;
- autonomous write-back or clinical action sold before it is independently governed and proven safe.

## Incentive test

Reject a revenue model if CareOS earns more when:

- clinicians open fewer sources only because verification is discouraged;
- safety/review barriers are bypassed;
- more PHI is copied centrally than the workflow needs;
- patients receive more interventions regardless of appropriateness;
- uncertainty is hidden to make the workflow look faster;
- a hospital becomes harder to leave because its clinical truth has been trapped in a proprietary system.

## Value metric

The commercial north star is:

> **Time Returned to Care — safety gated.**

A commercial win requires measurable workflow value **without worse error, provenance, source verification, privacy or clinician authority**.

## Open architecture / paid operation

CareOS should keep connector contracts, truth/provenance semantics and safety boundaries inspectable. Revenue comes from:

- dependable deployment;
- integration;
- specialty/workflow configuration;
- terminology/policy maintenance;
- assurance evidence;
- support/operations;
- repeatable hospital adoption.

The moat should be **trusted execution and accumulated integration/evidence know-how**, not owning the patient's record.
