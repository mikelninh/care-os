# V10 direction — Patient & Family View

Planned V10 audience for patients and authorised family/caregivers.

Design goals:
- plain-language patient timeline;
- "what changed since last time?";
- medication and allergy overview;
- upcoming appointments and open follow-ups;
- documents in original + understandable summary;
- questions to ask the care team;
- permissioned sharing / proxy access;
- IPS export/import for portable summaries;
- multilingual presentation while preserving original clinical source text.

Real patient-data testing should begin locally/de-identified until identity, authorisation, audit, encryption, retention and live-data governance gates are implemented.
