# CareOS — Verification

## Strong evidence today
- synthetic E2E journeys;
- adversarial agent/tool scenarios;
- lifecycle/provenance tests;
- outage/recovery behaviour;
- interactive clinical and patient-facing synthetic demos;
- FHIR/HL7 research integration paths;
- release-assurance and evidence matrices.

## Explicitly unproven
- qualified clinician accuracy on representative real workflows;
- production KIS/LIS integration;
- production PHI operation;
- multi-hospital repeatability;
- external clinical/privacy/security/regulatory assurance.

## Next proof gate
Real-World Proof Sprint 1: ≥5 complete safe paired clinician sessions for one workflow family, ≥3 independent reviewer perspectives, one real non-secret hospital capability manifest, and every external finding recorded as supported / falsified / blocked / unknown.

## Rule
No public claim may be stronger than the evidence ledger behind it.
