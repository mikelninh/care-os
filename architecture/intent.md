# CareOS — Intent

## Problem
Healthcare workers act as human middleware across fragmented EHR/KIS, LIS, RIS/PACS, documents, ePA and communication channels. Reconstructing trustworthy context costs attention that should return to care.

## Outcome
Help clinicians reconstruct changed, pending and critical context and prepare bounded documentation work with less search/reconciliation friction **without losing provenance, uncertainty or human authority**.

## North star
**Time Returned to Care — safety gated.** A speed gain is not a win if verification, provenance or safety gets worse.

## Users
- clinicians reviewing and documenting care;
- hospital IT/integration teams;
- later, patients/families consuming source-linked context.

## Non-goals
- autonomous diagnosis or treatment;
- replacing source systems;
- production PHI operation without approved governance;
- presenting synthetic evidence as clinical validation.
